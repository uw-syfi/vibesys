===== dynamic-orchestrator =====
Schedule at most 2 new workstreams for free slots. 0 other
workstreams are still running (their history rows show non-terminal phases), and the
run may schedule 2 more workstreams in total, including these.

Objective (authoritative, including operator constraints):
Improve.

Measurement: only the framework's trusted evaluation produces performance and
accuracy numbers that count. It runs on the target hardware, which need not be this
machine, so local device probes say nothing about the target. Never report a number
that did not come from a trusted evaluation.

Assign work only where it can succeed: never assign edits to read-only inputs, and
never plan work that needs target hardware outside trusted evaluation.

Current base revision (best trusted candidate, or the root): `fake-revision`
Your workspace holds the run's input, not candidate work: a workstream's files exist
only at the `revision` in its history row (read them with git show REVISION:PATH).

Fill every free slot: a slot left free idles until a running workstream finishes. When
no further mechanism is ready to implement, use a slot for independent work that
reduces uncertainty for the others, such as measuring or profiling the base revision.
Choose workstreams that investigate distinct mechanisms or competing hypotheses.
Each workstream is an independently useful line of inquiry. Continue an existing
hypothesis only when its recorded next step remains the best use of capacity.
A workstream's `evidence` may cite skill reference files that bear on its task;
implementers read their evidence files.
Do not split one implementation into parallel fragments and do not assign duplicate
hypothesis IDs. New IDs must not occur in the history. A continued workstream must
reuse its existing ID and set `continue_hypothesis`.
A hypothesis whose outcome is `blocked` may be continued only with a changed task
that removes the blocker its summary records; otherwise park or abandon it.
Use `hypothesis_updates` to park or abandon completed directions when new evidence
makes that strategic decision clear. Do not retire an unfinished direction.

The framework provides isolated candidate workspaces, runs selected independent
reviews, and owns trusted evaluation and winner adoption. Every candidate that an
implementer nominates and the review passes gets a trusted evaluation; only
evaluated, accepted candidates can be adopted or become the base revision. Near the
end of the budget, choose work that can produce a reviewable candidate in one workstream.
